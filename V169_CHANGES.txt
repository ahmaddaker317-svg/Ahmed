ADT Stock V169
================

1. Daily Sales permissions
- Daily Sales home card is visible to all users.
- Added member permission: can_manage_daily_sales.
- Allowed members can view and add daily sales.
- Settlement/reset remains main-manager only.

2. Home team card
- Team/Permissions card is hidden on the home screen for members and sub-managers.
- Main manager/developer still sees it.

3. Shortages
- Member shortage permission is now used for viewing and adding shortages.
- The “تم التوفير” delete button is hidden from members.
- Server-side adt_shortage_remove is restricted to the main manager/developer only.

4. Supabase
- Added V169_MEMBER_SALES_SHORTAGES_PERMISSIONS.sql.
- Added can_manage_daily_sales to team_members.
- Updated permission save/read RPCs and daily sales RPCs.
