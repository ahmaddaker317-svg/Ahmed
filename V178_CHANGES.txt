ADT Stock v178 — Debt share text + image

- Replaced the old forced WhatsApp transaction receipt action with two in-app share choices: text and image.
- Customer debt message now uses the approved wording: "تم تسجيل دين جديد بقيمة ..." and "إجمالي المبلغ عليك".
- Customer payment message now uses the approved wording: "تم استلام مبلغ ... وسداد جزء من الدين السابق".
- When the balance reaches zero, the payment message says "الحساب خالص" automatically.
- Added a formal white receipt/image generated inside the app with store name, customer, amount, date/time, details, balance and signature area.
- Added Android native Share Sheet support for text and PNG images; no forced WhatsApp destination.
- Historical receipts calculate the balance immediately after that transaction, so later movements do not change the old receipt balance.
- No new Supabase SQL is required for v178.
